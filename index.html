The real problem

Planning a proposal in NYC is overwhelming.
It doesn't have to be.

Central Park has 843 acres. Weekend crowds can ruin any intimate moment. Golden hour lasts 40 minutes and changes by the week. And the fear of being discovered before the ring comes out keeps most people up at night.

I've photographed 100+ proposals across New York. I know every hidden corner, every lighting window, every spot where the crowd disappears. You tell me your date — I build the entire plan around it.

"I have no idea where in Central Park to do it."
I scout 2–3 exact locations for your date, time, and the light conditions. You just choose the one that feels right. Location scouting is included.
"What if she sees the photographer?"
I position myself 30–50 feet away with a long lens. To her, I'm just another person in the park. She'll never know I was there — until she sees the photos.
"I don't know how to get her to the spot naturally."
I help you craft a believable "cover story" and timing plan. We rehearse the logistics over WhatsApp until you feel completely confident. Zero winging it.
